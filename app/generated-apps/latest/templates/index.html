Game Overview

Nine Men's Morris is an ancient strategy board game that dates back over 3,000 years. Two players compete to form "mills" (three pieces in a row) while trying to capture their opponent's pieces.

The game consists of three distinct phases: placement, movement, and flying. Each phase requires different strategies and tactical thinking.

  • Automatic win condition detection

How to Play

1

Placement Phase

Players take turns placing their 9 pieces on empty intersections of the board, trying to form mills while blocking the opponent.

2

Movement Phase

After all pieces are placed, players move pieces to adjacent positions to form mills and capture opponent pieces.

3

Flying Phase

When a player has only 3 pieces left, they can move to any empty position on the board, giving them increased mobility.
